## Study TTA scoring

The uploaded study checklist explicitly shows:

- Total Points: number of Yes / 28
- Total Critical Tasks Completed: number / 19
- Pass/Fail threshold: score ≥ 80%

For this reason the app reports the 80% study threshold and the critical-task result separately. It does not silently convert the TTA into the baseline app's previous 75%-plus-no-critical-failure rule.

## Individual-skill scoring

The selected CMC individual-skill checklists state that proficiency requires passing all critical tasks marked `C`. The TQ, NPA, NDC, and BLOOD assessment modules therefore preserve that critical-task gate.

## Validation note

The May 2026 CMC abbreviated TTA is retained as a validation source for clinical consistency. Because several selected individual-skill source sheets are dated 2020–2021, any protocol difference identified during PI/AI validation should be resolved through the study's formal content-control process rather than changed silently in the software.
